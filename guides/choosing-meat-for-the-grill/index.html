Guide · Grill

Which meat for the grill and how much per person

On the grill meat cooks fast over a strong fire, so it needs a little fat and a cut that does not turn tough. Lean breast and leg need a marinade and attention; neck, shoulder and chicken thighs forgive mistakes.

In short

  1. For mince (ćevapi, patties) take meat with about 20 % fat — beef shoulder, neck or brisket.
  2. For skewers: pork neck, boned chicken thighs, or chicken breast in a marinade.
  3. For steaks: sirloin, rib-eye or chuck, 2–3 cm thick.
  4. Allow 250–300 g raw meat per adult when the grill is the main course.
  5. Buy the meat a day ahead so it has time in the marinade or salt.

Cuts for long cooking — shin, stewing beef, ribs full of connective tissue — stay tough on the grill. Keep them for the pot, or slice them thin and marinate overnight.

What to look for at the butcher

Colour and smell

Beef is dark red, pork pink, chicken pale pink. The meat must not be sticky or grey and must not smell sour. Fat is white to cream, never yellow and greasy.

Minced meat

Best choose a piece and ask the butcher to mince it in front of you, twice: first through the coarse plate, then the fine one. That way you know what is in it and how much fat. Use ready-minced meat the same day.

Steak thickness

Grill steaks should be 2–3 cm thick. Thinner ones dry out before they get a crust; thicker ones need indirect heat.

Fish

Fresh fish has clear, bulging eyes, red gills and firm flesh that springs back when pressed. Ask for it to be gutted but scaled only if you will not grill it in its skin — the scales protect the flesh.

When something goes wrong

The ćevapi are dry and crumbly
The meat was too lean or not kneaded enough. Next time take a fattier cut (about 20 %) and knead until the mixture turns sticky.
The steak is tough
It was a cut for stewing, not grilling, or it was sliced along the grain. Slice across the grain and thinly.
The chicken is dry
The breast was cooked too long. Use thighs, or marinate the breast in yoghurt and cook until 74 °C in the middle, no longer.

Storage

Keep raw meat in the fridge at 0–4 °C, on the bottom shelf, covered. Use pieces within 2–3 days, mince and chicken within a day. Thaw frozen meat slowly in the fridge overnight, never on the counter.
