Grill marinades: what goes in and how long

A marinade flavours the surface of the meat and stops it drying out. Only salt goes deep; oil, spices and garlic stay on the outside — so a few hours are enough, not days.

In short

  1. 100–150 ml marinade is enough for 1 kg of meat.
  2. Base: oil + spices + garlic; a little acid if you like (lemon, vinegar, yoghurt).
  3. Marinate in the fridge in a covered dish or a bag and turn the meat once or twice.
  4. Chicken and pork 2–12 hours, fish only 15–30 minutes.
  5. Wipe off the excess before grilling — it drips, flares and burns.

How long

MeatAt leastAt mostNote
Chicken1 hour24 hoursin yoghurt best 4–12 hours
Pork (neck, loin)2 hours24 hoursovernight is ideal
Beef (steaks)—12 hoursgood steaks need only salt, oil and pepper
Lamb1 hour12 hoursrosemary, garlic, lemon
Fish15 min30 minlemon “cooks” the flesh if left longer
Squid15 min1 hourno acid, just oil and garlic
Vegetables15 min2 hourssalt aubergine first

Acid (lemon, vinegar, wine) and yoghurt tenderise only the surface. Leave meat in them too long and the outside goes mushy, so keep to the times above.

When to salt

Salt either at least 40 minutes before grilling, or right before the meat goes on. Salt 10–20 minutes ahead and it draws juice to the surface that the meat has not yet reabsorbed — the crust struggles to form. For skewers and steaks it is safest to salt at the end; for chicken the salt can go straight into the marinade.

Six basic marinades (for 1 kg meat)

1. Basic, for pork and beef

5 tbsp oil, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp sweet paprika, ½ tsp pepper. Finely chopped onion if you like.

2. Yoghurt, for chicken

150 ml thick yoghurt, juice of ½ lemon, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp paprika, 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp salt.

3. Lemon and rosemary, for lamb and chicken legs

4 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 4 garlic cloves, 1 tsp dried rosemary or two fresh sprigs, pepper.

4. Honey and paprika, for wings and ribs

3 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp honey, 2 tsp sweet paprika, a pinch of hot, 3 garlic cloves, juice of ½ lemon. Honey burns fast — cook over indirect heat and move over the coals only at the end.

5. Beer and onion, for pork neck

200 ml lager, 1 onion in rings, 3 tbsp oil, 1 tsp pepper, a teaspoon of mustard. Overnight.

6. For fish and squid

4 tbsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, a handful of parsley. Lemon only once the fish is cooked.

Step by step

  1. Pat the meat dry — a marinade clings better to a dry surface.
  2. Mix the marinade in a dish or a bag, add the meat and massage it in well.
  3. Squeeze the air out of the bag or cover the dish and put it in the fridge.
  4. Take the meat out of the fridge half an hour before grilling.
  5. Wipe off the excess marinade, salt if needed and grill.

When something goes wrong

The outside of the meat is mushy
It sat too long in lemon, vinegar or yoghurt. Keep to the times in the table, marinate fish without acid.
The marinade burns and smokes
Too much oil, sugar or honey on the surface. Wipe off the excess and cook part of the time beside the coals.
The flavour does not come through
It needs salt. Salt carries flavour — without it even the best marinade does not help.

Safety

Always marinate in the fridge, not on the counter. Do not use a marinade that held raw meat as a sauce — if you want to pour it over, boil it for 3 minutes first. Put cooked meat on a clean plate, not the one that held it raw.
