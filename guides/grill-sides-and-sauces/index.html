Sides and sauces for the grill

Grilled meat does not need much: a warm flatbread, chopped onion and something creamy — kajmak, ajvar or urnebes. One fresh salad takes the heaviness off the meat.

In short

  1. Warm the flatbread briefly on the grate and dip it in the meat juices.
  2. With beef and pork: chopped onion, kajmak, ajvar, urnebes.
  3. With chicken, fish and vegetables: garlic yoghurt, lemon, chard.
  4. One fresh salad in season: tomato or chopped salad with cheese in summer, cabbage salad in winter.
  5. Make the sides before you light the fire — when the meat is ready, nobody waits.

Make before the fire

Onion

Chop the onion finely or into thin half-moons, salt it and squeeze with your hands — it turns milder. For onion salad add a tablespoon of vinegar and oil.

Urnebes

Crumble white cheese with a fork with a little kajmak, garlic, hot and sweet paprika and a tablespoon of oil. If it is too hot, stir in some ajvar.

Garlic yoghurt

Thick yoghurt, crushed garlic, salt, a tablespoon of olive oil and, if you like, grated and squeezed cucumber and dill.

Roasted peppers

Grill the peppers until the skin blackens all over, put them in a covered bowl for 10 minutes, then peel. Dress with oil, vinegar and garlic.

Flatbreads

At the end of grilling split the flatbreads, put them briefly on the grate and brush with the juices from the plate the meat rested on. Refresh shop flatbreads by sprinkling them with water before warming.

Potatoes in foil

Wrap small potatoes in foil and put them straight into the embers for 40–50 minutes, turning. Split them open and add kajmak and salt.

When something goes wrong

The onion is too sharp
Salt and squeeze it once chopped, or soak it in cold water for five minutes and drain.
The urnebes is too runny
Too much oil or kajmak. Add more crumbled cheese.
The flatbreads are hard
They were on the fire too long. Warm them only half a minute a side, or wrap them in foil at the side of the grill.
