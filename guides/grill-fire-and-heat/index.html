Fire and coals: how to cook on the grill

You do not grill over flames but over coals covered in grey ash. With a hot side and a cooler side on the grill you can cook anything — from ćevapi in eight minutes to chicken legs in forty.

In short

  1. Light the charcoal in a chimney starter or on a few firelighter cubes — no petrol or spirit.
  2. The coals are ready when covered in grey ash, after 20–30 minutes (wood 45–60 minutes).
  3. Spread the coals on one side only: there you cook fast, beside them you finish slowly.
  4. Heat the grate well, clean it and oil it before the meat goes on.
  5. Rest the cooked meat 3–10 minutes before cutting.

Direct and indirect heat

Direct means over the coals: fast, a strong crust, for pieces under 3 cm thick. Indirect means beside the coals, best with the lid on: the grill works like an oven, for meat on the bone and thicker pieces. Easiest is to spread the coals over two thirds of the grill and leave one third empty — that is where meat goes when it flares or when it is done outside but not inside.

Step by step

1. Light the coals

Fill a chimney starter with charcoal, put crumpled newspaper or two firelighter cubes underneath and light. When the top coals turn grey, tip them into the grill.

2. Make two zones

Spread the coals over two thirds of the base, thicker along one edge. Put the grate on and leave it 5 minutes to heat.

3. Clean and oil the grate

Brush the hot grate clean, then wipe it with a cloth or half an onion dipped in oil. The meat will not stick.

4. Cook and leave it alone

Put the meat on and let it sear. Turn it when it comes away from the grate by itself — if it sticks, it is not time yet. Do not press patties with the spatula: you squeeze the juice into the fire.

5. Rest

Put the cooked meat on a clean plate or board. Ćevapi and skewers 2–3 minutes, steaks 5 minutes, big pieces 10 minutes. The juices settle back into the meat.

When something goes wrong

Flames lick the meat
Fat is dripping. Move the meat to the cooler side until the flames die down. Do not douse with water from a bottle — it throws ash onto the meat.
The meat sticks to the grate
The grate was not hot, clean or oiled enough, or you turned too early. Wait until it lets go by itself.
Burnt outside, raw inside
The heat is too strong for that thickness. Move the meat to the indirect side and put the lid on to finish.
The coals die before you finish
Too little fuel or the vents closed. Add charcoal at the edge in good time and keep the bottom vents open.

No grill: a grill pan

Everything also works in a heavy ridged pan or a cast-iron pan: heat it hard for 5 minutes, oil the meat (not the pan) and open a window. Times are the same as over hot coals; finish meat on the bone in the oven at 200 °C.

Safety

Keep the grill outdoors, away from walls and trees, never indoors or in a garage — charcoal gives off carbon monoxide. Keep children and pets away. Deal with the ashes only when completely cold, best the next day, and never in a plastic bin.
